COUNTRY: United States
AUTHORITY: FDA
REGULATION: 21 CFR Part 312
SECTION: § 312.6
TITLE: Labeling of an investigational new drug

SOURCE:
https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-312/subpart-A/section-312.6

SOURCE VERSION:
October 15, 2026

REGULATORY TEXT:

(a) The immediate package of an
investigational new drug intended for
human use shall bear a label with the
statement “Caution: New Drug—Limited
by Federal (or United States) law to
investigational use.”

(b) The label or labeling of an
investigational new drug shall not
bear any statement that is false or
misleading in any particular and
shall not represent that the
investigational new drug is safe or
effective for the purposes for which
it is being investigated.

(c) The appropriate FDA Center Director
may grant certain exceptions or
alternatives under the conditions
specified in § 312.6(c).
